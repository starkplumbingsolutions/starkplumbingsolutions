Repair decisions

Repair, replace, or investigate further?

Some jobs are straightforward component replacements. Others involve concealed damage, aged pipes, incompatible parts or upstream causes that need testing. Consider these points before authorizing work:

  • Water at the base can come from condensation or a compromised seal
  • Repeated clogs may be caused by drain restrictions rather than the toilet
  • A cracked fixture should not be handled like a routine gasket repair

Describe the symptom, not just the part you think is broken. An informed assessment can protect against replacing a functioning fixture while leaving the original issue unresolved.
