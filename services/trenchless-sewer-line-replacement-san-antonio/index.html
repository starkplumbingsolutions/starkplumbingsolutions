What Is Trenchless Sewer Replacement?

Trenchless sewer work repairs or replaces underground pipe through small access points instead of digging a long trench. Instead of removing a driveway, mature trees or landscaping, work is done from one or two small excavations or existing access points.

It is not magic, and it is not right for every line. The pipe has to be a candidate, which is why inspection comes first.

Signs a Sewer Pipe May Need Replacement

  • Slow or gurgling drains throughout the house.
  • Persistent sewage odors inside or outside.
  • Wet, green or soggy patches over the sewer path.
  • Frequent backups that return even after snaking or jetting.
  • Camera findings of cracks, root intrusion, offsets or corrosion.

San Antonio has a lot of aging cast iron and clay pipe, and shifting clay soil adds stress. See Sewer Line Repair for more.

Pipe Bursting and Pipe Lining Explained

These are the two main trenchless approaches, and they are not the same thing.

Pipe bursting and pipe lining compared
Pipe burstingPipe lining (CIPP)
What happensA new pipe is pulled through while the old pipe is broken outwardA resin-coated liner is cured inside the existing pipe
ResultA brand new pipeA pipe within the old pipe
Best forSeverely damaged or deteriorated pipeCracks, leaks and root entry in pipe that still holds its shape
LimitsNeeds entry and exit pointsCannot fix a belly, collapse or incorrect slope
Typical reported costAbout $150 to $300 per linear footAbout $80 to $250 per linear foot

Pipe lining is not the same as replacement. It rehabilitates the existing pipe. Which methods fit your line is confirmed after inspection.

Sewer Inspection and Eligibility

A camera answers three questions: where the damage is, how bad it is, and whether the pipe still has the shape and slope a trenchless method needs. We also locate depth and path so the plan is accurate.

If the camera shows trenchless will not work, we tell you before you spend money on it. See Sewer Camera Inspection.

Installation and Property Restoration

Work is done from small access points, so restoration is far smaller than a full trench. We explain what will be dug, what will be restored and what is outside the scope before work starts. Anything promised is on the estimate.

After the work, we camera the line again to confirm the result.

What Does Trenchless Sewer Replacement Cost in San Antonio?

Reported San Antonio ranges: lining about $80 to $250 per foot, pipe bursting about $150 to $300 per foot, and full trenchless projects from about $1,900 to $12,000 or more.

The spread is wide because length, depth, access and pipe condition drive everything. Homeowners in forums report quotes from several providers that differ by thousands. Ask each one for the method, the footage and what restoration is included, and compare those, not just the total.

What moves the price

  • Line length and depth.
  • Method: lining or bursting.
  • Number and difficulty of access points.
  • Pipe condition, which decides eligibility and scope.
  • Permits and restoration.

How to avoid overpaying

  • Ask for the camera footage behind the quote and confirm whether the quote covers a post-work camera check.
  • Ask for the price in writing before work starts, with labor, parts and any trip or diagnostic fee listed separately.
  • Ask the plumber to explain the cause in plain words. If you get a vague answer, get a second opinion.
  • Compare what is included, not just the headline price. A cheap special that grows into a long add-on list is not cheap.
  • Never let scope change on the fly. If something new is found, work should stop until you approve it.
  • Ask what happens if the problem comes back, and make sure anything promised is on the estimate.
Ranges are published and reported San Antonio market figures, not a Stark quote. Your written estimate gives the exact price.

Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement FAQs

Can trenchless methods replace every sewer pipe?

No. Trenchless works well on cracks, root intrusion and aging pipe that still holds its shape. It generally cannot fix a belly, correct incorrect slope or repair a collapsed section. A camera inspection tells you whether your line is a candidate.

What is pipe bursting?

Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward into the surrounding soil. The result is a brand new pipe in the same path with minimal digging.

Is pipe lining the same as replacement?

No. Lining places a resin-coated liner inside the existing pipe and cures it to form a new surface. It rehabilitates the old pipe and cannot fix a belly or collapse. Pipe bursting installs a new pipe.

What are the disadvantages of trenchless pipe replacement?

It cannot fix every problem, it needs a pipe that is a candidate, and it is not always cheaper. The camera and a clear conversation about the options keep you from paying for the wrong method.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Yes. You get a written estimate listing labor, parts and any trip or diagnostic fee, and we wait for your approval. If we find something that changes the scope, we stop and talk to you first.
