Can frozen pipes burst after thawing?

Yes. A frozen pipe often cracks while frozen and only starts to leak as it thaws, when water flows again. If a pipe is frozen, thaw it gently with warm air, never an open flame, and be ready to shut off the main valve. Stark Plumbing Solutions repairs frozen and freeze-damaged pipes in San Antonio, TX. Call (210) 898-9754.

What to do if a frozen pipe bursts

  1. Shut off the main valve. Do it first, especially if you see water or a crack.
  2. Open the lowest faucet. Drain remaining water from the system.
  3. Thaw gently if intact. Warm air from a hair dryer, heat tape or warm towels. Start near the faucet and work back. Never use a torch or open flame.
  4. Keep the heat on. Open cabinet doors to let warm air reach pipes on exterior walls.
  5. Call Stark. Call (210) 898-9754. Even a thawed pipe may be cracked and should be checked.
Never use an open flame, torch or high-heat gun on a frozen pipe. It can cause steam pressure that ruptures the pipe, or start a fire.

What people worry about most, answered

“Why is there no water during a freeze?”

A frozen pipe blocks flow. Check which fixtures are affected to narrow down where.

“Will it burst when it thaws?”

It can, if it already cracked. That’s why we inspect, not just thaw.

“Everyone’s calling plumbers.”

Cold snaps are busy. Call early, describe the problem and text photos so we can sort what’s urgent.

“Is it covered by insurance?”

Freeze damage is often covered when sudden and accidental, but policies differ. Keep photos and your estimate for your insurer.

Why Texas freezes can damage plumbing

Many San Antonio homes have pipes in attics, garages, exterior walls and outdoors with little insulation, because hard freezes are rare. When one hits, water inside those pipes freezes and expands. See outdoor plumbing for exposed lines.

Preventing future pipe freezes

  • Insulate exposed pipes in attics, garages and crawlspaces
  • Cover outdoor faucets and disconnect hoses
  • Know where your main shut-off is and test that it turns
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during a freeze
  • Let a faucet drip during a hard freeze

Frequently asked questions

Can frozen pipes burst after thawing?

Yes. A pipe can crack while frozen and only leak once water flows again. Be ready to shut off the main valve.

What should I do if no water comes from a faucet during a freeze?

Keep the faucet open, check other fixtures, and gently warm exposed pipes with warm air. Do not use a flame. If it doesn’t flow or you see a leak, call (210) 898-9754.

When should I call a plumber?

Call if you see a crack, wet walls, water after thawing, no water for long, or if the frozen pipe is inside a wall or slab.

How do I safely thaw a frozen pipe?

Use a hair dryer, heat tape or warm towels, starting near the faucet. Never use an open flame, torch or high-heat gun.

Does insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Often when sudden and accidental, but it varies by policy. Keep photos and written estimates.

How do I prevent frozen pipes in San Antonio?

Insulate exposed pipes, cover outdoor faucets, open cabinets on exterior walls and drip a faucet during hard freezes.
