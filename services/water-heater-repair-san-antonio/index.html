Common Water Heater Problems

Most calls come down to five patterns: no hot water, hot water that runs out quickly, a leak at the base or a fitting, rusty or discolored hot water, and noise from the tank. Each points to different parts, so the same symptom can have very different causes and costs. A tripped breaker, a failed upper element, a dead pilot or a stuck drain valve can all look alike from the faucet.

No Hot Water and Temperature Issues

Electric tanks have two elements and two thermostats. If the upper element fails you may get lukewarm water that never fully heats. If the lower one fails, hot water runs out after a few minutes. Gas units depend on a working pilot or electronic ignition, a thermocouple or flame sensor, and clean burner air. Check the breaker and the thermostat setting first. If the unit still will not light or heat, call us rather than adjusting gas controls yourself.

Leaking Water Heaters

A leak from the tank body usually means the tank has failed, and repair rarely makes sense. A drip from the temperature and pressure (T&P) relief valve, or from the inlet and outlet fittings, is often fixable. If the tank is actively leaking, shut off the water supply to the heater and keep the area clear until a plumber arrives.

Gas and Electric Water Heater Problems

Gas units can produce carbon monoxide when venting or combustion air is wrong, so a yellow flame, soot, or a headache in the home are reasons to call right away. Electric units run on 240-volt power, and a failed element or wiring fault can cause shock or fire. Both types need a licensed technician for diagnosis.

Sediment and Mineral Buildup

San Antonio's hard water leaves minerals on the tank floor. Sediment cuts heating efficiency, makes the tank rumble or pop as water boils beneath the layer, and can shorten the life of the elements. A professional flush during service can restore performance and is often the cheapest fix for a noisy unit.

Water Heater Repair vs. Replacement

A useful rule of thumb: if the unit is around 10 years old or older and the repair costs more than about half of a new unit, replacement usually wins. Leaks from the tank body are almost always replacement cases. We put both options in writing so you can choose with the numbers in front of you.

Before the Plumber Arrives

Having these ready shortens the visit and helps us quote accurately.

  • Note the brand, model and manufacture date from the label
  • Write down the fuel type: gas, electric or tankless
  • Describe exactly what changed and when it started
  • Photograph any puddle, drip, rust stain or error code
  • Clear the path to the unit and the space around it
  • List any earlier repairs or flushes

Seasonal Issues in San Antonio

  • Summer: heavy hot water use and high ambient heat strain tanks. Schedule a flush before peak season.
  • Fall: test the T&P valve and check for drips before cold months and holiday guests.
  • Winter: a garage or attic unit can lose heat fast during a power or gas interruption.
  • After storms: power surges burn out elements. Ask for an inspection after a major outage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my water heater not producing hot water?

The usual causes are a tripped breaker or failed element on electric units, a pilot or ignition problem on gas units, or a thermostat set too low. A technician checks each in order and gives you a written estimate before any part is replaced.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

Leaks from the tank body generally mean replacement. Leaks from the T&P valve, drain valve or fittings can often be repaired. We confirm which it is before recommending a fix.

Why is my hot water discolored?

Rust-colored water usually comes from corrosion inside the tank or a depleted anode rod. Brown or gritty hot water can be sediment. A flush or replacement resolves most cases.

How much does water heater repair cost in San Antonio?

Minor repairs such as a thermostat or element cost a visit fee plus parts. Larger repairs and replacements cost more. You receive a written estimate before work starts, and we call you before any change in scope.
