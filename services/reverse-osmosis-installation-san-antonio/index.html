Short answer

A reverse osmosis (RO) system filters drinking water at one point of use, usually under the kitchen sink. Installation connects the cold supply, a drain saddle, a dedicated faucet and a storage tank. RO does not replace a whole-house softener for hardness. Filters are changed on a schedule, and the membrane lasts longer.

How Reverse Osmosis Systems Work

Water passes through pre-filters, then a semi-permeable membrane, then often a carbon post-filter, and is stored in a small tank. Rejected water goes to the drain. RO is excellent for drinking water, but it is a point-of-use system. It does not treat the whole house.

Under-Sink RO Installation and Faucet Connections

  1. Mount the filters and tankUnder the sink, with room for filter changes.
  2. Drill the faucet holeGranite, quartz and stainless need the right bit and care. Local homeowners call this the hardest part of a DIY install.
  3. Connect supply and drainA feed adapter on the cold line and a drain saddle or air gap on the drain.
  4. Start up and testWe flush the system, check for leaks and set the tank pressure.

Common RO System Leaks

  • Push-fit tubing not fully seated
  • Drain saddle not sealed
  • Tank valve or housing O-ring
  • Overtightened plastic fittings that crack

Filter and Membrane Replacement

Pre-filters are typically replaced more often, and the membrane lasts longer. Follow the manufacturer’s schedule and the condition of your water. Hard water shortens membrane life, which is why pairing with a softener helps.

Reverse Osmosis Installation FAQs for San Antonio Homeowners

Can reverse osmosis connect to an existing sink?

Yes. We can drill for a dedicated faucet or use an existing hole if suitable.

What maintenance does an RO system require?

Replace pre-filters on schedule, replace the membrane as needed and sanitize periodically.

Does reverse osmosis replace the need for a softener?

No. RO is for drinking water at one tap. A softener treats hardness for the whole house.

How long does an RO install take?

A standard under-sink install usually takes a couple of hours.

Can RO feed my fridge?

Yes, with a dedicated line and the right tubing.

Why is my RO system leaking?

Usually a loose push-fit connection, a drain saddle or a cracked fitting.
