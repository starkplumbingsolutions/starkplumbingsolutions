How to Choose the Right Plumbing Service

Many San Antonio homeowners tell us the hard part is not the repair, it is finding a plumber they can trust on price. Use this checklist with any company, including us.

  • Get a written estimate. It should list labor, parts and any diagnostic fee before work starts.
  • Verify the license. Look up the plumber with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners and ask for proof of insurance.
  • Ask what was found. A good plumber explains the cause and your options, without pressure.
  • Beware of surprise add-ons. If the scope changes, work should stop until you approve it.
  • Match the specialist to the problem. Hidden leaks, sewer lines and gas work call for the right tools and licensing.
Smell gas or see sewage flooding? Leave the area, avoid switches and flames, and call your gas utility or 911 first. For water emergencies, shut off the main valve, switch off the water heater and then call us.

Need help paying for an emergency leak repair?

The San Antonio Water System offers a Plumbers to People program for qualifying residents. Call 210-704-SAVE or visit saws.org. Stark is not affiliated with SAWS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of plumbing services are listed?

This directory lists 50 dedicated plumbing services in San Antonio, TX across nine groups: emergency plumbing, drain, sewer and leak services, bathroom and kitchen fixtures, water heaters and water lines, gas and water treatment, outdoor drainage, pumps and septic, new construction and remodeling, and commercial plumbing.

How can I find the right plumbing service for my problem?

Use the problem picker or search box at the top of this page. Choose your symptom, such as no hot water, a slow drain, low pressure or a hidden leak, and it points to the matching service pages. Not sure? Call (210) 898-9754 and describe what you see.

Which services address clogged drains and sewer backups?

Drain Cleaning, Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning, Toilet Clog Removal, Hydro Jetting, Sewer Camera Inspection, Sewer Line Repair and Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement. If several fixtures back up at once, the problem is usually in the main line.

Where can I find water heater installation or repair?

The Water Heater, Water Line and Repiping section lists Water Heater Repair, Water Heater Installation and Replacement, and Tankless Water Heater Services, plus water line repair and whole-house repiping.

Do you list plumbing services for remodeling and new construction?

Yes. New Construction Plumbing, Plumbing Rough-In, Bathroom Remodel Plumbing and Kitchen Remodel Plumbing each have a dedicated page.

Which services require specialist providers?

Gas line repair, backflow testing, septic work and sewer replacement often require specific licensing or permits. We explain what your job needs before work begins. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility or 911 first.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Yes. You get a written estimate listing labor, parts and any trip or diagnostic fee, and we wait for your approval. If we find something that changes the scope, we stop and talk to you first.

How do I check that a plumber is licensed in Texas?

Ask for the license number and look it up with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners at tsbpe.texas.gov. Also ask for proof of liability insurance before any work begins.
