What Is Hydro Jetting?

Hydro jetting pushes water at high pressure through a hose and a specialized nozzle inside the pipe. The water streams scour the pipe wall in a full circle, flushing grease, scale and debris out of the line. A cable only pokes a hole through a clog, and jetting actually cleans the pipe.

It is a cleaning method, not a repair. If a pipe is damaged, jetting will not fix that, which is why the first step is looking inside.

Problems Hydro Jetting Can Address

  • Grease buildup coating kitchen and main lines.
  • Mineral scale narrowing the pipe, a common problem with San Antonio hard water.
  • Tree roots entering through joints, in pipe that is otherwise sound.
  • Recurring clogs that return after a cable.
  • Heavy debris and sludge in long runs.

It is not a fix for a cracked pipe, a collapsed section or a bellied line that holds water. Those need a repair, see Sewer Line Repair.

If a simple clog is the problem, a cable is the right answer and we will say so. See Drain Cleaning.

Camera Inspection Before Jetting

Plumbing forums are full of homeowners who were quoted $1,100 to $1,700 for scoping plus jetting and could not tell whether that was fair. A camera look before jetting protects you in two ways: you see what is in the line, and you find out if the pipe is a good candidate.

One practical note from plumbers: a camera is not useful in a pipe that is completely full of debris, since the screen shows nothing. So the usual order is to clear, inspect, then decide whether jetting is needed. See Sewer Camera Inspection.

Hydro Jetting for Residential Drains

Homes with recurring main line clogs, older cast iron with scale, or root problems are the usual candidates. Access matters: a cleanout near the house makes jetting far easier, and if you do not have one, ask us about adding one.

Many San Antonio homes built decades ago have older pipe. That does not rule out jetting, but it means we inspect first and tell you honestly what the pipe can take.

Commercial Drain and Grease Buildup

Restaurants, cafeterias and shops that put grease and food into their drains build up scale quickly. Regular jetting on a maintenance schedule can keep lines flowing and avoid emergency backups during service hours.

What Does Hydro Jetting Cost in San Antonio?

Residential jetting is usually priced higher than a cable clearing because it takes more equipment and time. Homeowners in San Antonio forums report quotes from roughly $1,100 to $1,700 when scoping is included, and many are unsure if that is reasonable. We give you the price in writing after the camera shows what is in the line, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

What moves the price

  • Length and diameter of the line.
  • Amount of grease, scale or roots.
  • Access: whether there is a cleanout or we must use another access point.
  • Whether a camera inspection is included, before or after the job.

How to avoid overpaying

  • Ask whether the quote includes a camera look before and after, and whether you get to see the footage.
  • Ask for the price in writing before work starts, with labor, parts and any trip or diagnostic fee listed separately.
  • Ask the plumber to explain the cause in plain words. If you get a vague answer, get a second opinion.
  • Compare what is included, not just the headline price. A cheap special that grows into a long add-on list is not cheap.
  • Never let scope change on the fly. If something new is found, work should stop until you approve it.
  • Ask what happens if the problem comes back, and make sure anything promised is on the estimate.

Hydro Jetting FAQs

Can hydro jetting remove grease buildup?

Yes. High-pressure water scours grease and scale from the pipe wall instead of just punching a hole through it, which is why jetting is a common choice for kitchen lines and restaurant drains.

Is hydro jetting appropriate for old pipes?

It depends on the pipe. Sound older pipe can often be jetted, but cracked, collapsing or badly corroded pipe may be damaged by high pressure. That is why we inspect with a camera before we jet and tell you if it is not a good candidate.

How is hydro jetting different from drain snaking?

A snake or cable breaks through or pulls out a clog and leaves buildup on the walls. Jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the pipe wall in a full circle, which removes grease, scale and roots more thoroughly.

Do I need a camera inspection before jetting?

We recommend it. It shows what is in the line and whether the pipe is sound enough to jet. If the line is completely blocked, we may need to clear it first so the camera can see.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Yes. You get a written estimate listing labor, parts and any trip or diagnostic fee, and we wait for your approval. If we find something that changes the scope, we stop and talk to you first.
