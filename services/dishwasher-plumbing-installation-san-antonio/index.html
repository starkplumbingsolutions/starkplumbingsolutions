Dishwasher Plumbing Hookup Services

We connect new and replacement dishwashers to water and drain, then run a cycle to check for leaks.

Dishwasher Water Supply Connections

A proper hot-water supply line and shut-off make installation and future service safe. We replace old or corroded lines and valves.

Drain Hose and Air Gap Requirements

The drain hose needs a high loop or air gap so dirty water cannot flow back into the dishwasher. We follow local requirements and connect to the disposal or drain correctly.

Shut-Off Valves and Supply Lines

A dishwasher with no dedicated shut-off is a risk if a leak occurs. We install a 3/8-inch compression valve and a durable braided line.

Plumbing for Replacement Dishwashers

Replacing an older unit often reveals corroded valves or poor drain routing. We update these so the new unit is protected.

Common Dishwasher Connection Leaks

Leaks come from loose inlet fittings, cracked hoses and disposal inlet connections, and can rot cabinets. We find and fix the connection, not just wipe up water.

Dishwasher Plumbing Installation FAQs

Does a dishwasher need a dedicated shut-off valve?

Yes. A dedicated valve lets you stop water to the dishwasher without shutting off the whole sink, and makes service safer.

Who connects a dishwasher water line?

A licensed plumber can connect the water supply and drain. Electrical hookup is separate and may need an electrician.

Why is water leaking beneath my dishwasher?

Usual causes are a loose or cracked inlet supply line, a worn drain hose connection or a failed door seal. Turn off the valve and call us to locate it.

Will you install a dishwasher I bought at a big-box store?

We handle the plumbing side of connecting it. Appliance and electrical work are separate.
