Commercial Plumbing Services in San Antonio

Quick answer: Commercial plumbing covers the repair, maintenance, testing and installation of water, drain, sewer, gas-adjacent and fixture systems in non-residential buildings. Commercial plumbing is not residential plumbing with bigger pipe. Buildings have longer drain runs, more fixtures on one stack, higher water demand, code-required devices and tenants or customers who notice every outage. A slow drain in a house is an annoyance. In a restaurant on a Friday night, it is lost revenue and a possible health-code problem.

Why small commercial leaks get expensive

A dripping joint in a mechanical room is easy to ignore. Over a few weeks it can rot drywall, corrode hangers and feed mold in a space no one looks at. Texas heat adds another layer: expanding and contracting clay soil moves slab-supported and underground lines, and mineral-heavy water attacks fittings from the inside. We recommend replacing the failing component and checking its neighbors, rather than returning for the next joint in a month.

Urgent situations

If water is actively spreading, shut the main valve, move valuables and call us. For a burst line, see our emergency plumber page.

Commercial Drain and Sewer Services

Short version: If more than one fixture drains slowly, or a floor drain gurgles, the problem is usually the main line, not the fixture. Snaking the fixture rarely helps. Camera inspection finds the cause.

Commercial drains carry heavy loads: restaurant grease, paper products, wipes, food solids, mop water and tenant waste. When the line is shared by several suites, one tenant's habits can back up another's space.

Camera inspection

A waterproof camera shows breaks, roots, bellies, offsets and buildup. We locate the problem depth and position so repairs are scoped precisely. Learn about sewer camera inspection.

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water scours grease, scale and roots from pipe walls, where a cable only bores a hole. Ideal for restaurant lines and recurring clogs. See hydro jetting.

Sewer line repair

Spot repairs, sectional replacement or trenchless options depending on camera findings, access and site constraints. See sewer line repair.

Warning signs of a main line problem

Checked two or more? Call us for a camera inspection before the backup happens during business hours.

Do not DIY this

Store-bought drain chemicals can damage older pipe and are a hazard for staff. Rental cables can snag on a broken line and make the break worse. Sewer gas and sewage carry health risk. A line that backs up in a food-service space can trigger closure until it is resolved properly.

Commercial Water Heater and Supply Systems

Hot water is non-negotiable for restaurants, salons, medical offices and apartment buildings. Our water heater repair team works on tank systems, tankless units and recirculation loops.

San Antonio draws heavily on the Edwards Aquifer, and the water is hard. Scale builds in tanks and on heating elements, insulating the water from the heat and shortening equipment life. Flushing, anode checks and softening make a real difference.

Supply side problems we solve

  • Pressure issues: a worn pressure regulator can let high pressure hammer fixtures and valves, or restrict flow at the far end of the building.
  • Slow hot water at fixtures: usually a recirculation pump, check valve or insulation issue rather than the heater itself.
  • Scale and sediment: reduced flow, noisy tanks and early element failure.

Seasonal issues in San Antonio

☀ Summer

Dry, shifting soil stresses underground lines. Peak water use exposes weak pressure and slab leaks. Check irrigation backflow and look for unexplained water-bill jumps.

🌧 Storms

Heavy rain can overload sewers and cause pressure changes. Cleanouts and backwater protection matter. Inspect after major storms.

❄ Winter freezes

Hard freezes are rare here, so buildings are often unprepared. Insulate exposed lines, backflow assemblies and outdoor spigots. See frozen pipe repair.

🍂 Fall

Best time to schedule inspections, water heater flushes and backflow testing before holiday traffic.

Commercial Plumbing Maintenance and Inspections

Why maintain? Planned maintenance costs less than emergency repair. It also protects uptime, reduces water waste and gives you documentation for insurers and inspectors.

Backflow testing

Backflow devices keep contaminated water from flowing back into the public supply. Most commercial properties with irrigation, fire lines, boilers or certain equipment have testable assemblies that must be tested by a licensed tester. Our backflow testing service tests, repairs and documents the result so you can submit it on time.
