What Gas Line Repair Covers (and What It Does Not)

We work on gas piping after the meter: black iron, CSST and approved flexible connectors that feed your water heater, furnace, range, dryer, grill and fire features.

CPS Energy owns the meter and the utility side. If CPS shut your gas off, a licensed plumber fixes the customer-side piping, then CPS restores service. This is the most common point of confusion in San Antonio threads, so we explain it up front.

CPS Shut Off My Gas: What Happens Next

  1. Do not remove tags or locksUtility locks and tags are there for safety. Tampering can delay restoration and create danger.
  2. Call a licensed plumberWe find the leak with approved methods, repair or replace the piping and document the work.
  3. Pressure testThe system is tested to confirm it holds before gas is turned back on.
  4. Request restorationCPS Energy restores service once repairs are complete and any required inspection is done.

Gas Line Repair, Replacement and Installation

Repair

Leaking joint or fitting

Cut out the failed section and replace it with proper fittings. Patching a threaded joint with tape or sealant is not a repair.

Replace

Corroded or damaged pipe

Old black iron can corrode, and soil or moisture can attack buried pipe. Replacement is often safer than repeated repairs.

Install

Appliance hookups

New range, dryer, tankless heater or outdoor kitchen with a shut-off valve at each appliance.

Install

Outdoor gas lines

Grills, fire pits and pool heaters need buried or protected piping, correct depth and a locate before digging.

Gas Pressure Testing and Inspection

After any repair or new run, the piping should be pressure tested and checked at every joint. A test is how we prove the work holds, not an upsell. When a permit or inspection applies, we coordinate it.

San Antonio notePermit and inspection rules for gas work depend on the job and the City of San Antonio. We confirm requirements before starting and list them in your estimate.

What Affects Gas Line Repair Cost

Cost depends on access, pipe length, pipe material, trenching or slab work, number of appliances and permits. Any price you see online without a visit is a general range, not a quote.

FactorWhy it matters
AccessAttic, crawl space, slab or yard digging changes labor
Length and sizeLonger or larger lines cost more pipe and time
MaterialBlack iron, CSST or buried pipe each need different methods
Permits and testingRequired for many jobs and included in a written estimate

Two quotes that look different often leave out different things. Ask whether testing, permits, shut-off valves and restoration of drywall or soil are included.

Why Gas Lines Fail Here

San Antonio has many older homes with decades-old black iron, shifting clay soil and caliche that can stress buried piping, and a lot of outdoor living with grills and fire features added after the original build. Appliance swaps are a common trigger: a new range or water heater exposes an old connector or undersized line.

Why You Should Not DIY a Gas Line Repair

Why this is not a DIY jobGas leaks can cause fire, explosion and carbon monoxide risks. In Texas, gas piping work must be done by licensed plumbers.
  • Soap and sealant do not make a code-compliant repair.
  • A hidden leak can stay undetected without testing.
  • Wrong pipe size starves appliances or creates unsafe pressure.
  • Unpermitted work can cause insurance and resale problems.

Gas Line Repair & Installation FAQs for San Antonio Homeowners

Can a licensed plumber repair gas piping?

Yes. A licensed plumber can repair, replace and install gas piping after the meter. For a suspected active leak, leave and call 911 and CPS Energy first.

Do gas line installations require permits?

Often yes. Requirements depend on the job and the City of San Antonio rules. We confirm permits before work and list them in the estimate.

Who handles gas pressure tests?

A licensed plumber tests the customer-side piping after repair or installation. CPS Energy restores service after repairs are complete.

How much does gas line repair cost in San Antonio?

It depends on access, length, material and permits. A firm price needs a visit. We give a written estimate before work and you approve it first.

CPS shut off my gas. What do I do?

Do not remove tags or locks. Call a licensed plumber to find and repair the leak and pressure test the piping, then ask CPS Energy to restore service.

Can I add a gas line for a grill or fire pit?

Yes. We size the line, install a shut-off valve, check locates and permits, and test the line before use.
