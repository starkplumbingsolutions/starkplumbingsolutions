What counts as a plumbing emergency?

A plumbing emergency is any problem that is causing damage right now or making your home unsafe or unusable: a burst pipe, a broken water line, a sewer backup, a leaking water heater or no water at all. If water is still running, shut off the main valve first, then call a plumber. Stark Plumbing Solutions answers at (210) 898-9754 for homes and businesses in San Antonio, TX.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  1. Shut off the water. Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. It is usually near the water meter, at the front of the house, or where the line enters the garage or utility room.
  2. Cut power near water. If water is near outlets, appliances or the breaker panel, turn off power only if you can reach the switch without standing in water. Otherwise stay clear and call.
  3. Open a low faucet. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain pressure from the pipes and slow the flow.
  4. Take photos and move valuables. Photos help with insurance. Move rugs, electronics and furniture away from the water.
  5. Call Stark at (210) 898-9754. Tell us what you see and where. Text a photo if you can. We’ll tell you what we think is going on and the next step.
Gas smell? Leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not use switches or phones inside. Sewage or water touching electrical? Stay out of the room.

Emergency water shut-off and damage prevention

Knowing where your main shut-off is can save thousands in damage. Find it today: it is usually near the water meter in the front yard or where the line enters the house. Turn it fully clockwise. If it will not turn or leaks, call us. If the leak is on the street side of the meter, call SAWS at 210-704-7297.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Any problem causing damage or making your home unsafe or unusable: burst pipes, major leaks, sewer backups, a leaking water heater, or no water. If water is spreading, shut off the main valve and call (210) 898-9754.

What should I do before a plumber arrives?

Shut off the main water valve, avoid standing water near electrical, open a low faucet to relieve pressure, take photos for insurance, and move valuables away from the water.

How do I shut off water during a major leak?

Find the main shut-off valve, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it clockwise until it stops. If the leak is before the meter, call SAWS at 210-704-7297.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Yes. Stark gives a written estimate before work begins and starts only after you approve it.

How can I check that a plumber is licensed?

Search the plumber’s name or license number at tsbpe.texas.gov, the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners.

I rent. Who do I call?

Call your landlord or property manager’s emergency maintenance line first. They usually need to authorize repairs.

Can I get help paying for a leak repair?

The San Antonio Water System runs a Plumbers to People program for eligible low-income homeowners. Call 210-704-SAVE to ask about eligibility.
