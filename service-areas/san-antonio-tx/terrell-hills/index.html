Winter plumbing in Terrell Hills

Hard freezes are rare in San Antonio, which is why they cause so much damage: many pipes in attics, garages and exterior walls have little insulation. February 2021 left thousands of homes with burst lines. Insulate exposed pipe, disconnect hoses, cover outdoor faucets and know where your main shutoff is.

For larger established and custom homes: Large homes have more exterior wall and attic pipe. Insulate exposed runs before a freeze and know every shutoff.

Spring plumbing in Terrell Hills

When not to do it yourself

Many homeowners are handy, and a few jobs are fine to do yourself, such as replacing a toilet flapper, cleaning a faucet aerator or plunging a single sink. These jobs carry real risk of flooding, gas exposure, injury or a denied insurance claim.

  • Gas lines and gas water heatersA bad connection leaks gas and requires a pressure test by a licensed plumber.
  • Sewer lines and main drainsRental augers can tear old pipe and turn a clog into a repair.
  • Slab leaksGuessing the location means breaking the floor in the wrong place.
  • Water heater replacementVenting, relief valves and gas or electric hookups carry fire and scald risk.

  • Repeated chemical drain cleanersHarsh chemicals can damage old pipe and are dangerous for the next person who works on the line.

Check for a hidden leak in 5 minutes

A simple meter test finds many hidden leaks without any tools. First, turn off every faucet, appliance and irrigation zone, and make sure no one is using water. Next, find your water meter, usually in a box near the street or curb, and lift the lid carefully. Look at the small leak indicator, often a triangle or dial, and note the reading. If the indicator moves while everything is off, water is escaping somewhere. Wait fifteen minutes and check again. If the reading has changed, close the main shutoff and see whether the movement stops. If it stops, the leak is inside the house. If it continues, the leak is between the meter and the house. Either way, do not start digging or breaking into walls. Call a licensed plumber so the leak can be located and repaired without extra damage, and photograph the meter readings for your records.

Plumber in Terrell Hills: frequently asked questions

Can a sewer camera identify recurring drainage problems?

Slow drains in more than one fixture, gurgling and sewer smell point toward the main line rather than a single fixture. Roots, belly sections, joint separation and grease build-up are common causes. A sewer camera inspection shows the cause so you can choose between cleaning, hydro jetting or repair. Cleaning without looking often leads to the same clog returning.
