Quick answer

The Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) 2026-27 pays ₹2,500 per month to boys and ₹3,000 per month to girls who are wards of ex-servicemen or ex-Coast Guard personnel (below officer rank) and have started the first year of an eligible professional degree course with at least 60% in their qualifying exam. Apply online on the KSB portal (online.ksb.gov.in) before 31 December 2026. The award is renewable every year for the length of the course, up to five years.

What is the Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)?

The Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) is a central government scholarship for the dependent children and widows of ex-servicemen and ex-Coast Guard personnel. It was introduced from the academic year 2006-07 to encourage higher technical and professional education among the families of those who served the country. The scheme is run by the Kendriya Sainik Board (KSB) under the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare, Ministry of Defence, and it reaches students through the Rajya Sainik Boards (state level) and Zila Sainik Boards (district level).

Every year KSB selects 5,500 wards and widows, split equally between boys and girls (2,750 each). Because the number of awards is fixed and applications usually exceed it, PMSS is merit based. Selection follows a priority order that favours families of personnel killed or disabled in service, then ranks candidates on marks.

PMSS is often confused with other schemes that carry the words "Prime Minister's Scholarship". For example, there are separate schemes for Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles, for the Railways, and for wards of state police personnel martyred in terror or Naxal attacks. Those are applied for through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The ex-servicemen scheme described on this page is applied for on the KSB portal.

PMSS scholarship amount and total value

PMSS is a fixed-rate scholarship. It does not change with the fees of your college, and the scheme pages mention no separate fee reimbursement. Girls receive a higher rate than boys, and the amount is paid once a year rather than monthly.

Understanding the "below officer rank" rule

PMSS is designed mainly for the families of personnel below officer rank, which in practice means other ranks such as sepoys, naiks, havildars and junior commissioned officers, and their equivalents in the Coast Guard. Children of commissioned officers are generally outside the scheme. The exception is when the officer died in harness or was disabled because of a cause attributable to service, in which case rank does not matter. If your situation sits near this line, ask your Zila Sainik Board for a written clarification before you apply.

What "first year only" really means

PMSS offers a fresh award only once, at the start of the course. If you took admission in 2026 you apply in the 2026-27 cycle. Students already in the second year, or who joined through lateral entry, cannot apply for a fresh award. If you miss the cycle for the year you joined, you cannot apply in a later year. That is why early application matters more than it appears.

Tip: the 60% rule uses your minimum educational qualification (MEQ), not your Class 10 marks. For most degree courses that is your Class 12 result.

Eligible and non-eligible courses under PMSS

PMSS supports professional degree courses recognised by a government regulator such as AICTE (engineering, technology, management), the National Medical Commission (medicine), the Pharmacy Council of India, the Indian Nursing Council and the Bar Council of India. The test is not the name of the course but whether it is a regulated professional programme.

How PMSS selection works: priority categories

Because there are 5,500 awards, KSB ranks applications using six priority categories. Candidates in higher categories are considered first, and the awards are divided equally between boys and girls. KSB's published pages do not explain exactly how candidates are ranked inside a category, so treat marks as the main lever you control.

PMSS priority order
PriorityCategoryProof usually needed
1Wards or widows of personnel killed in actionSupporting service documents
2Disabled in action and boarded out, with disability attributable to serviceMedical board / disability documents
3Died in service from causes attributable to serviceSupporting service documents
4Disabled in service with an attributable disabilityMedical board / disability documents
5Recipients of gallantry awardsAward certificate with Gazette notification
6Other personnel below officer rankCopy of the PPO

Most applicants fall under category 6. If your family belongs to a higher category, upload every supporting document, because a missing certificate can push you down to category 6.

How to apply for PMSS 2026-27 step by step

The whole process is online on the KSB portal. Paper applications are not accepted. Allow around 45 minutes if your documents are already scanned, and a few extra days to collect the Zila Sainik Board certificate and bonafide certificate if you do not have them yet.

  1. 1

    Check eligibility and collect originals

    Confirm the service status of the ex-serviceman, your 60% in the minimum educational qualification, first-year admission and course recognition. Keep original certificates ready because the Zila Sainik Board checks scans against originals.

  2. 2

    Register on the KSB online portal

    Open online.ksb.gov.in. The ex-serviceman, widow or orphan fills Parts 1 and 2 with PPO number, identity card number, Aadhaar, bank account, mobile number, email ID, dates of enrolment and discharge, and uploads a photograph.

  3. 3

    Log in and start a New Application

    Use the login ID and password sent to your registered email. Open Application and Renewal Application, choose New Application, and fill in the online form. One candidate can apply for one course only.

  4. 4

    Upload the required documents

    Upload the ex-serviceman certificate (Annexure-1), bonafide certificate, Aadhaar-linked bank certificate (Annexure-3), matriculation certificate, MEQ certificate, passbook page and PPO or category documents.

  5. 5

    Click Save and Forward

    Submit the form with Save and Forward. An application that still shows the status New has not been submitted.

  6. 6

    Submit before 31 December 2026

    Apply as soon as admission is confirmed rather than waiting for the deadline. Late applications are not accepted.

  7. 7

    Respond to verification observations

    The Zila Sainik Board or Coast Guard HQ checks documents within five working days and the Rajya Sainik Board does a second check. Correct any observation within 10 days or the system rejects the application automatically.

  8. 8

    Track the merit list

    Log in to track status. The merit list is prepared by a Board of Officers at KSB and uploaded on the KSB website, with new applications processed between January and May.

Verification, observations and the merit list

After you submit, your application passes through three checks. First, the Zila Sainik Board (or Coast Guard HQ for Coast Guard cases) compares every uploaded document with the originals, usually within five working days. Second, the Rajya Sainik Board performs a second-level check and recommends eligible applications to KSB. Third, KSB processes the application, and a Board of Officers finalises the merit list, which is uploaded on the KSB website.

If a checking authority raises an observation, for example an unclear scan or a missing signature, you have 10 days to correct it. An observation that is not corrected in time leads to automatic rejection by the system. Check your registered email and the portal status regularly, and keep your mobile number and email active for the entire course, because KSB sends alerts there.

PMSS renewal and payment rules

Winning the award is only the first step. To keep receiving PMSS you must renew it each year and keep your academic record clean. The first year's amount is remitted in advance after selection. Later payments follow each completed academic year.

Renewal conditions
RuleDetail
Minimum marksAt least 50% in the year just completed
ContinuityNo break in studies
BacklogsFailing any subject or semester can end the scholarship permanently
FormOnline Payment-cum-Renewal Form on the KSB portal
Supporting papersBonafide certificate, mark sheets and a bank passbook page
DeadlineWithin one year of completing the academic year
Record keepingRecord the date and amount of each payment on the KSB portal

Older state-level help pages advise students not to send renewal applications between January and May, because the PMSS team is busy preparing the merit list for first-year students. Plan your renewal for the rest of the year.

Common mistakes and rejection reasons

Most rejections are avoidable. The list below is drawn from the KSB rules and the way the verification process works.

  1. Applying in the second year or later. Only first-year admissions qualify for a fresh award.
  2. Marks below 60% in the MEQ. There is no relaxation mentioned in the eligibility rules.
  3. Leaving the form at "New". If you never clicked Save and Forward, nobody will verify it.
  4. Ignoring observations. The 10-day correction window is strict.
  5. Choosing a non-eligible course. MA, MSc, MCom, diplomas, distance and foreign courses are excluded.
  6. Applying as a ward of a paramilitary or civilian employee. Those wards are outside the KSB scheme.
  7. Bank account problems. The account must be in the student's name and linked to Aadhaar.
  8. Unreadable or mismatched documents. Names, dates of birth and service numbers must match across documents.
  9. Missing the renewal. Late or skipped renewals delay payment and can end the award.
  10. Exceeding the combined scholarship cap. PMSS plus other assistance cannot exceed the PMSS amount.

Expert tips to improve your chances

  • Apply in the first month of admission. Early applications leave time to fix observations and avoid last-minute portal congestion.
  • Visit the Zila Sainik Board in person early. The certificate in Annexure-1 is usually the slowest document to arrange.
  • Use one consistent spelling. Names on the Aadhaar, mark sheets, PPO and bank records should match exactly.
  • Upload full-page, legible scans. Include stamps and signatures, and keep each file within the portal's size limits.
  • Save the registration email. It contains your login credentials, which you need for renewals for the entire course.
  • Do not rely on estimated dates from third-party sites. Check the KSB notice, then add a buffer of at least two weeks.
  • Maintain your marks. The 50% renewal threshold is easy to lose track of in a demanding course such as engineering or medicine.
  • Declare other scholarships honestly. The combined-amount cap exists, and mismatches can cause problems.

FAQs: PM Scholarship (PMSS) 2026-27

What is the last date to apply for PM Scholarship (PMSS) 2026-27?+
The Kendriya Sainik Board (KSB) notice for academic year 2026-27 gives 31 December 2026 as the last date for fresh applications. Fresh applications opened on 1 August 2026. Do not wait for the last day, because your Zila Sainik Board must verify your documents and any observation has to be fixed within 10 days.
How much money does PMSS give per month in 2026-27?+
Boys receive ₹2,500 per month (₹30,000 per year) and girls receive ₹3,000 per month (₹36,000 per year). The amount is paid annually, not month by month, for the number of years the course lasts (two to five years).
Who is eligible for the PM Scholarship Scheme for ex-servicemen wards?+
Dependent wards and widows of ex-servicemen and ex-Coast Guard personnel below officer rank are eligible. Wards and widows of personnel who died in harness or were disabled due to service-attributable causes are eligible irrespective of rank. The ward must have scored at least 60% in the minimum educational qualification and must be in the first year of an eligible professional course.
Is there an income limit for PMSS?+
No income limit is stated in the KSB eligibility rules for PMSS. Eligibility depends on the service status of the ex-serviceman, the ward's marks, age and course, not on family income. An income certificate is not part of the standard document list.
Should I apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or the KSB portal?+
Fresh PMSS applications for wards of ex-servicemen and ex-Coast Guard personnel are made on the KSB online portal (online.ksb.gov.in), and paper applications are not accepted. NSP lists separate Prime Minister's Scholarship schemes for other groups, such as Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles, Railways and state police martyrs.
What is the minimum percentage required for PMSS?+
You need at least 60% in the minimum educational qualification (MEQ). The MEQ is Class 10+2 for most degree courses, a diploma for lateral-entry style routes where applicable, or graduation for MBA and MCA.
Can second-year or third-year students apply for PMSS?+
No. Only students who took admission to the first year in the current academic year can apply for a fresh award. Lateral-entry students and students in the second or later years are not eligible for a fresh application.
Which courses are covered under PMSS?+
Professional degree courses recognised by a government regulator such as AICTE or the National Medical Commission, for example BE/BTech, MBBS, BDS, BPharm and BSc Nursing. Integrated courses such as BSc-BEd and BA-LLB are eligible with 10+2 as the MEQ. At postgraduate level only MBA and MCA are covered. Diploma, distance, vocational and foreign courses are not covered.
Is MSc, MA or MCom covered under PMSS?+
No. PMSS is meant for professional and technical education. Pure arts, commerce and science postgraduate courses are not covered. The only postgraduate courses accepted are MBA and MCA.
How many children of one ex-serviceman can get PMSS?+
The scholarship is admissible for a maximum of two wards of one ex-serviceman. A widow can also apply in her own right, subject to the age rules.
What is the age limit for PMSS?+
A male ward must not be older than 25 years at the time of admission. A female ward remains eligible until she marries. A widow has no age limit until she remarries.
Are wards of CAPF or paramilitary personnel eligible under KSB PMSS?+
No. The KSB scheme states that wards of civilians, including paramilitary personnel, are not eligible. Separate Prime Minister's Scholarship schemes exist for Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles and for the Railways, and you apply for those through NSP.
How many students get PMSS every year?+
KSB selects 5,500 wards and widows each academic year, split equally between boys (2,750) and girls (2,750). Selection is by merit within priority categories, so applying early and with correct documents matters.
When is the PMSS money credited?+
The first year's amount is remitted in advance, after selection, to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account. Later payments follow each completed academic year after you submit the online Payment-cum-Renewal Form with your mark sheets and bonafide certificate.
How do I renew PMSS every year?+
Log in to the KSB portal after completing the academic year and submit the online Payment-cum-Renewal Form, a bonafide certificate, mark sheets and a bank passbook page. You need at least 50% marks and no break in studies. Failing any subject or semester can end the scholarship permanently.
Can I get PMSS and another scholarship together?+
Only up to a limit. KSB rules say the total of PMSS and any other scholarship or assistance cannot exceed the amount available under PMSS. Check the rule before accepting a second scholarship, and declare other assistance honestly.
How do I contact KSB for PMSS help?+
The KSB PMSS helpline is listed as 011-26715250. Always cross-check contact details on ksb.gov.in, and for document verification approach your Zila Sainik Board first because it handles the first-level check.
